servings: "1"
ingredients:
  - 2 tsp instant coffee (see shopping notes below)
  - 1 cup (240ml) water
  - 1/2 cup (120ml) unsweetened almond milk, cold from the fridge
  - 1 scoop vanilla protein powder (the scoop that comes in the tub)
  - Pinch of cinnamon (optional)
  - Ice cubes, if making it iced
notes: Never stir protein powder directly into hot coffee — it clumps and can curdle. Always dissolve it in the cold almond milk first, then combine.
---

## What to buy (one-time shopping)

- **Instant coffee** — this is coffee that's already brewed and dried into granules, so you just add hot water. No machine, no grinder, no filters. Look for a small jar labelled "instant coffee" (Nescafé Gold, Starbucks Via, or any store brand). **Don't** buy "ground coffee" or "coffee beans" — those need brewing equipment you don't have yet.
- You already have the vanilla protein powder and almond milk. That's everything.

**Equipment you need:** a kettle or any small pot to boil water, a mug, a spoon, and a jar with a tight lid (or a protein shaker bottle if you have one).

## Steps

1. **Boil water.** Fill a kettle or small pot with about 1½ cups of water and heat until it's bubbling. If using a pot, turn off the stove once it boils.
2. **Let the water calm down for ~30 seconds.** Water straight off a full boil scorches instant coffee and makes it taste bitter. Just-stopped-bubbling is perfect.
3. **Make the coffee.** Put 2 teaspoons of instant coffee granules into your mug. Pour in 1 cup (240ml) of the hot water and stir for a few seconds until no granules are visible. It should look dark brown, almost black — don't worry, the next steps mellow it out. That's it: you've made coffee.
4. **Shake the protein milk.** In a jar with a lid (or shaker bottle), combine ½ cup cold almond milk and 1 scoop of vanilla protein powder. Screw the lid on tight and shake hard for 15–20 seconds until it's completely smooth with no lumps. No jar? Whisk vigorously with a fork in a bowl — it takes a minute longer.
5. **Let the coffee cool slightly.** Wait 1–2 minutes after making it. It should be hot, but not scalding — this stops the protein from curdling in the next step.
6. **Combine.** Slowly pour the protein-milk mixture into the coffee while stirring with a spoon. It'll turn creamy and pale brown, like a latte.
7. **Taste and adjust.** Vanilla protein powder is usually sweet enough on its own. Too strong or bitter? Add a splash more almond milk. Want it cozier? Add a pinch of cinnamon on top.

## Iced version (great for summer)

1. Make the coffee as in steps 1–3, but use only ½ cup of hot water so it's extra strong.
2. Let it cool for 5–10 minutes (or pop it in the fridge).
3. Fill a tall glass with ice, pour the coffee over, then top with the shaken protein milk from step 4. Stir and enjoy.

## Troubleshooting

- **Lumpy or grainy drink?** The protein powder hit hot liquid. Next time shake it into the cold milk first and let the coffee cool a bit longer.
- **Too bitter?** Use 1½ teaspoons of instant coffee instead of 2, or add more almond milk.
- **Too weak?** Use a heaping 2 teaspoons, or reduce the water to ¾ cup.
