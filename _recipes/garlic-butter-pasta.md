---
title: Garlic Butter Pasta
tags: [pasta, quick, vegetarian]
time: 20 min
servings: "2"
ingredients:
  - 200g spaghetti
  - 4 tbsp butter
  - 4 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  - Parmesan, grated
  - Salt and pepper
  - Handful of parsley, chopped
notes: Save some pasta water — it's what makes the sauce silky instead of greasy.
---

1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water before draining.
2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and chili flakes, cook until fragrant (about 1 minute) — don't let the garlic brown.
3. Add the drained pasta to the pan with a splash of pasta water. Toss until the sauce clings to the noodles, adding more pasta water as needed.
4. Off the heat, stir in most of the Parmesan. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Plate up, top with the rest of the Parmesan and the parsley.
